About Practical AI Tools

New AI tools launch every week, and most "best of" lists just copy each other. We write plain-English guides that explain what each tool is actually good at, who it's for, and when you don't need to pay at all.

How we choose what to recommend

  • We compare tools on their real features, plans and limits, as published by each company.
  • We say so when a free option is good enough.
  • We update guides when tools change their features or pricing.
  • Affiliate commissions never decide our rankings. A tool that pays us more doesn't get ranked higher.
