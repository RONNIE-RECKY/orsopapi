# Short-form video scripts (TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts)

Each script is about 30–45 seconds. Record it as a screen recording of the tool with your
voice, or make it faceless with Fliki or InVideo AI. Put the site link in your bio, never
a raw affiliate link (TikTok and Instagram often limit reach for those).

Caption ending for all posts: **"Full guide at the link in bio. #AItools #sidehustle"**
Always disclose: add **#ad** or "affiliate link in bio" when a video promotes a tool you earn from.

---

**1. "Faceless YouTube channel in 10 minutes"**
Hook: "You don't need a camera or your voice to start a YouTube channel. Here's how."
Body: Type a topic into InVideo AI, show it generating script, scenes, voice. Edit one line.
Close: "I compared the 4 best AI video tools. Link in bio."

**2. "This voice isn't real"**
Hook: play 5 seconds of an ElevenLabs voiceover, then: "That voice is AI."
Body: show typing the script and picking a voice. Mention audiobooks, YouTube, ads.
Close: "Best AI voice tools compared, link in bio."

**3. "Stop paying for AI writing tools (unless you're one of these 3 people)"**
Hook: the title. Body: free chatbots are enough for most people; pay for Jasper (teams),
Frase (SEO), Writesonic (all-rounder). Close: "Which one are you? Full breakdown in bio."

**4. "The $0 online business stack"**
Hook: "5 free AI tools to start an online business this weekend."
Body: ChatGPT/Claude for ideas, Canva for design, Fliki for video, ElevenLabs for voice,
Systeme.io for your sales page. Close: "Step-by-step guide in bio."

**5. "Speak 10 languages without learning them"**
Hook: show a HeyGen translated clip. Body: explain video translation with lip-sync and who it's
for (coaches, creators, businesses). Close: "Best AI video tools, link in bio."

**6. "Blog post to TikTok in 60 seconds"**
Hook: "Turn any article into a video in a minute." Body: paste a blog URL into Fliki and show
the result. Close: "Guide in bio."

**7. "I asked AI to build me a digital product"**
Hook: the title. Body: chatbot writes an ebook outline, Canva template makes it look pro,
Systeme.io sells it. Close: "Full stack in bio."

**8. "AI tool tier list for creators"**
Hook: "Ranking AI tools for content creators." Body: quick S/A/B tiers with one-line reasons.
Close: "Detailed comparisons in bio."

**9. "Don't buy an AI video tool until you watch this"**
Hook: the title. Body: different tools fit different jobs (faceless = Fliki/InVideo,
avatar = HeyGen, company training = Synthesia). Close: "Pick yours, link in bio."

**10. "3 AI side hustles that actually work in 2026"**
Hook: the title. Body: faceless YouTube, AI voiceover services, digital templates, each with
the tool that powers it. Close: "How to start each one, link in bio."
