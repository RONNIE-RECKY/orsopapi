# Best AI Video Generators in 2026

AI video tools have gone from a novelty to something real businesses and creators use every day. You can now turn a script into a finished video with a presenter, voiceover, captions and stock footage without filming anything.

The catch: these tools are built for **different jobs**. Pick the wrong one and you'll pay for features you don't use. This guide sorts them by what you're actually trying to make.

## Quick answer

| If you want to... | Best pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Make faceless YouTube / TikTok videos fast | [Fliki](go:fliki) | Text or blog post to video with voiceover and stock footage |
| Generate a full video from one prompt | [InVideo AI](go:invideo) | Writes the script, picks scenes and adds a voice for you |
| Use a realistic talking avatar (or clone yourself) | [HeyGen](go:heygen) | Strong avatars, plus translation with lip-sync |
| Make training, onboarding or company videos | [Synthesia](go:synthesia) | Built for teams, many languages, polished presenters |

## 1. Fliki: best for faceless content creators

[Fliki](go:fliki) turns a script, idea, blog post or even a tweet into a video. It pairs your text with stock clips or images, reads it out with an AI voice, and adds captions.

**Good for:**
- Faceless YouTube channels (facts, history, motivation, listicles)
- Turning blog posts into short videos for TikTok, Reels and Shorts
- People who don't want to be on camera or use their own voice

**Watch out for:** the output looks like stock footage plus voiceover. That's fine for most faceless niches, but it won't feel like a cinematic edit.

## 2. InVideo AI: best "prompt to finished video"

With [InVideo AI](go:invideo) you describe the video you want, something like *"a 60-second YouTube Short about 5 money habits, upbeat tone"*, and it writes the script, picks scenes, adds a voiceover and music, and gives you a draft you can edit with plain text commands like "make the intro shorter".

**Good for:**
- Beginners who don't know how to edit video
- Pumping out lots of short-form videos quickly
- Ad variations and social content

**Watch out for:** you'll still want to review the script it writes. AI-written facts aren't always right.

## 3. HeyGen: best AI avatars and video translation

[HeyGen](go:heygen) is known for realistic talking avatars. You type a script and a lifelike presenter says it. You can also create an avatar of yourself, so you can "record" videos without filming each one.

Its other standout feature is **video translation**: upload a video of you speaking and it re-voices it in another language with matching lip movement. That's a big deal if you want to reach audiences in other languages.

**Good for:**
- Coaches, consultants and course creators who want to appear in videos without filming
- Sales and marketing videos
- Translating existing videos into other languages

<p class="cta"><a href="go:heygen">Try HeyGen free</a></p>

## 4. Synthesia: best for business and training videos

[Synthesia](go:synthesia) is aimed at companies: onboarding, training, product explainers and internal comms. It offers a large library of AI presenters, supports a wide range of languages, and has team features like shared workspaces and brand templates.

**Good for:**
- HR, training and customer-education teams
- Companies replacing slideshow training with video
- Anyone who needs polished, professional-looking presenters

**Watch out for:** it's more than a solo creator making TikToks needs. Fliki or InVideo will suit that better.

## How to choose in 30 seconds

1. **Don't want to show your face or use your voice?** Use Fliki or InVideo AI.
2. **Want a human-looking presenter?** Use HeyGen if you're a creator or small business, Synthesia if you're a company.
3. **Want to reach other languages?** Use HeyGen's translation, or Synthesia for multilingual training.

## A note on pricing

All four have a free tier or free trial at the time of writing, and paid plans are monthly or annual (annual is usually cheaper). Pricing and minute limits change often, so check the current plans on each site before you commit. **Start free, make 2–3 real videos, then upgrade only the tool you actually used.**

## FAQ

**Can I monetize AI-generated videos on YouTube?**
Yes, as long as the content adds value and follows YouTube's policies. Channels that mass-upload low-effort, repetitive videos risk being demonetized. Use AI to speed things up, but add your own ideas, research and structure.

**Do I need a powerful computer?**
No. All of these run in your browser, and the heavy processing happens on their servers.

**Which is cheapest to start with?**
Use the free plans first. Fliki and InVideo AI are usually the most budget-friendly for solo creators.
