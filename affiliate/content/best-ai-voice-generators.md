## Tips for voiceovers that don't sound like AI

1. **Write the way people talk.** Short sentences. Contractions. Questions.
2. **Use punctuation for pacing.** Commas and full stops create natural pauses, and an ellipsis (...) adds a beat.
3. **Generate a line twice** if it sounds off. Results vary slightly each time.
4. **Add background music quietly** under the voice. It hides small imperfections.

## Is it legal to use AI voices commercially?

Generally yes, on a paid plan. Most tools only grant commercial rights on paid tiers, so check the licence before using a free-plan voice in a monetized video or ad. Never clone someone else's voice without their permission.
