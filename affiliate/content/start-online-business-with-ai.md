# How to Start an Online Business With AI Tools

You no longer need a team, a big budget or technical skills to start an online business. AI handles much of the writing, design, video and voice work, so a single person can launch something that used to take five.

This guide gives you a **simple plan** and the **tool stack** to do it, starting from free.

## Step 1: Pick one thing to sell

Keep it simple. The three easiest models for beginners:

1. **A digital product**: an ebook, template pack, mini-course or planner
2. **A newsletter**: grow an email list around one topic, then earn from sponsors and products
3. **A content channel**: faceless YouTube or TikTok, monetized with ads and affiliate links

Choose a topic you can talk about for 100 posts: fitness for busy parents, budgeting, cooking on a budget, learning a skill, a local travel scene, and so on.

> Use a free AI chatbot (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini) to brainstorm: *"Give me 20 digital product ideas for [audience] who struggle with [problem]."*

## Step 2: Create the product with AI

- **Write it:** draft your ebook or course outline with an AI chatbot, then edit it in your own voice. For SEO blog content, a tool like [Frase](go:frase) helps you write articles that rank.
- **Design it:** [Canva](go:canva) has templates for ebooks, workbooks, slides and social posts, plus built-in AI design tools.
- **Make videos:** [InVideo AI](go:invideo) or [Fliki](go:fliki) turn scripts into videos. See our [AI video generator guide](/best-ai-video-generators/).
- **Add a voice:** [ElevenLabs](go:elevenlabs) for realistic voiceovers. See our [AI voice guide](/best-ai-voice-generators/).

## Step 3: Set up a place to sell

You need a sales page, checkout and email list. The simplest all-in-one option for beginners is [Systeme.io](go:systeme): funnels, email marketing, course hosting and checkout in one place, with a **free plan** to start.

If you mainly want to run a **newsletter**, [Kit](go:kit) (formerly ConvertKit) is built for creators and also lets you sell digital products.

Want your own website or blog instead? [Hostinger](go:hostinger) is a low-cost host with a simple website builder and WordPress hosting.

## Step 4: Get traffic for free

1. **Short videos** on TikTok, Reels and YouTube Shorts: post daily for 30 days and see what sticks
2. **Search**: answer specific questions your audience Googles, in blog posts or YouTube videos
3. **A lead magnet**: give away a free checklist or mini-guide in exchange for an email address, then sell to your list

## The honest truth

AI makes **creating** fast, but it doesn't replace **consistency**. The people who succeed pick one idea, ship something small within a week, and keep improving it for months. Start free, upgrade only when a tool is actually holding you back, and focus on talking to your audience.
